Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how the idore Creative Studio team handles information when you browse the Studio, make or review a video, or send an approved video to TikTok. It applies to the Studio hosted at yjr927.github.io/idore_tools.

Browsing and saving references

The discovery feed shows public TikTok references, including source links, creator handles when available, video descriptions, engagement figures and the dates we observed them. We use these details to credit sources and help people assess a reference. Opening an embedded original loads TikTok’s player, which receives information from your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the Studio and may log technical information such as your IP address when it serves a page.

Your browser stores saved references and records of workspace actions on this device. You can remove a saved reference or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Clearing browser data does not remove a file from the public gallery.

Creating and reviewing videos

The workspace processes the references, character images, motion guides, prompts, generated media, captions and review decisions used for a project. GitHub hosts the workspace and runs production jobs. Depending on the job, OpenRouter and the selected model provider, including OpenAI or ByteDance’s Seedance, receive the inputs needed to generate video, create images or suggest captions.

The materials library, production previews and completed videos can be publicly accessible. A finished video can appear in the Studio gallery before anyone approves sending it to TikTok. Only submit material suitable for this audience.

Sending a draft to TikTok

When the connection is enabled, an authorized reviewer can select an approved video and confirm the destination TikTok account. The upload uses TikTok’s video.upload permission. The workflow uses the configured account identifier, account label and access token to send the video and check the transfer. The account holder opens the upload in TikTok, adds or pastes a caption, chooses visibility and decides whether to publish.

TikTok receives the video for the authorized account. The Studio records the transfer status and identifiers needed to check delivery or prevent a duplicate. You can revoke the app’s access through TikTok’s connected-app settings. Manage copies already transferred to TikTok in your TikTok account.

In the JJ TEST sandbox, Cloudflare hosts the login and upload service. When you connect TikTok, the service reads your display name and account identifier, keeps your access token encrypted for up to 30 minutes, and sets a session cookie. You can preview the test video on your device. When you confirm the upload, the service forwards the video to TikTok and keeps a receipt associated with a hashed account identifier to prevent duplicate delivery.

Where records are kept

A GitHub credential entered in workspace settings stays in that browser tab’s memory for the session. Cloud production credentials are configured separately in GitHub Actions secrets. The production workflow sets a seven-day retention period for its TikTok upload receipt files. Public source records, review information and gallery files can remain in the project until removed. Repository history and copies others have saved may persist after a page changes.

Your choices and privacy requests

For privacy questions or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, email the Studio team at yjr927@gmail.com.

If your request concerns a public item, include its Studio link. We may ask for information to confirm that an account-related request comes from the account holder.
