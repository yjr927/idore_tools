Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how the idore Creative Studio team handles information when you browse the gallery, use the Agent to make videos, or connect your TikTok account. It covers the Studio on GitHub Pages and its JJ TEST sandbox on Cloudflare.

Browsing and saving references

The discovery feed shows public TikTok references, including source links, creator handles when available, video descriptions, engagement figures and the dates we observed them. We use these details to credit sources and help people assess a reference. Opening an embedded original loads TikTok’s player, which receives information from your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the Studio and may log technical information such as your IP address when it serves a page.

Your browser stores saved references and records of workspace actions on this device. You can remove a saved reference or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Clearing browser data does not remove a file from the public gallery.

Creating and reviewing videos

The workspace processes the references, character images, motion guides, prompts, generated media, captions and review decisions used for a project. GitHub hosts the workspace and runs production jobs. Depending on the job, OpenRouter and the selected model provider, including OpenAI or ByteDance’s Seedance, receive the inputs needed to generate video, create images or suggest captions.

The Agent can choose photos from the project’s materials library and use them to make a video. The library, production previews and completed videos can be publicly accessible on GitHub and in the Studio. Only submit material suitable for this audience.

Sending a draft to TikTok

Cloudflare hosts the sandbox sign-in and transfer service. When you connect TikTok, the service reads your account identifier and display name, sets a sign-in session cookie and uses TikTok’s video.upload permission. A separate protected cookie lets the gallery show receipts for your account. If you turn on automatic delivery, the Agent can send new videos made from library photos to your TikTok inbox. Earlier gallery videos and test clips are excluded from automatic delivery.

For automatic delivery, the sandbox stores encrypted TikTok access and refresh tokens on Cloudflare and renews them while authorized. Turning off automatic delivery removes these saved credentials and stops future transfers. The sandbox keeps a private receipt linked to a fingerprint of your account and video, with transfer identifiers, status and available timestamps to show progress and prevent duplicate uploads. The separate manual review workflow, when configured, uses the destination account and video confirmed by an authorized reviewer.

Opening a delivery notification in TikTok lets you edit the video, choose its visibility and decide whether to post. You can revoke the Studio’s access through TikTok’s connected-app settings. You can manage videos already transferred to TikTok in your TikTok account.

Where records are kept

A GitHub credential entered in workspace settings stays in that browser tab’s memory for the session. Cloud production credentials are configured separately in GitHub Actions secrets. The production workflow sets a seven-day retention period for its TikTok upload receipt files. Public source records, review information and gallery files can remain in the project until removed. Repository history and copies others have saved may persist after a page changes.
